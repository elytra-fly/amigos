ABOUT AMIGOS

About Amigos
16 Years of Excellence

CHANGANASSERY BRANCH

Sixteen years of quiet mastery. Since our doors first opened on College Road, Amigos has been the address Changanassery trusts for its most important days. A unisex salon and bridal academy where hygiene is ritual, craft is heritage, and every guest leaves a little more themselves.

Our styling floor is led by Senior Stylist Sonia, whose bridal artistry has dressed hundreds of Kerala brides — and whose academy now trains the next generation of makeover artists.

Founded in Changanassery, Amigos is a premier unisex salon and academy. We blend artistry with technique to bring out your best self.
